The Driskill

driskillhotel.com →

One of our favorite places in the world, The Driskill has been around since 1886, famously hosting LBJ and Lady Bird Johnson's first date in 1934.

We love the history, the architecture, the old-school oil baron vibes, and, of course, the bar. Rumor has it there are even some spirits wandering the halls, so keep an eye out for ghostly wedding crashers.

Mostly though, it's just a dang good hotel (and we know a lot about those), and they throw a killer party. We're pretty stoked to add our names to the guest book, and we look forward to celebrating here with all of you.

The Driskill
604 Brazos St, Austin, TX 78701 (Corner of 6th & Brazos)

The Driskill is right in the middle of downtown Austin, near the corner of 6th St and Congress, with an easy walk to a lot of our favorite spots.

Austin-Bergstrom Airport (AUS)
~15–20 Mins

Located approximately 10 miles southeast of downtown. Quick rideshare or taxi pickup directly from the terminal to the hotel's Brazos Street entrance.

San Antonio Airport (SAT)
~1 Hr 20 Mins

Located ~75 miles south via I-35 N. A great alternative airport option with extensive direct flights and straightforward rental car access.

Probably not. The Driskill is in the middle of downtown, not too far from the airport, and a lot of what you’ll want to do is within walking distance or easily accessible with a quick Uber or Lyft. If you’re planning to explore beyond downtown, though, or stick around for a while, then Austin is a city where having a car can come in handy.
