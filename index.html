The Driskill

driskillhotel.com →

The Driskill has anchored downtown Austin since 1886. It famously hosted LBJ and Lady Bird’s first date in 1934, survived over a century of Texas politics, and still runs one of the best bars in the city. We love the architecture, the history, the old-school oil baron vibes, and the unconfirmed ghost stories.

Mostly, it's just a great hotel that throws a great party. We're pretty stoked to add our names to the guest book, and we can't wait to celebrate here with all our favorite people.
